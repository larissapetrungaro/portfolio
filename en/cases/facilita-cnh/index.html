Facilita CNH · Driving lesson booking

I designed the flow for students to find an instructor, choose a package and time slots and pay in the app, making clear what happens at each step: confirmation, refunds and deadlines.

Context and problem

Facilita CNH (formerly Rota da CNH) is LM’s app for people getting their first driver’s license. It already offers practice tests for the written exam and is on the Play Store with 5K+ downloads and a 4.8 rating. The next step was to take students all the way to the driving lesson: find an instructor or driving school near home, choose a vehicle, package and time slots, and pay without leaving the app.

Until then, students booked driving lessons directly with a driving school. Government changes to Brazil’s new licensing rules reshaped that market, and the app started bringing instructors and driving schools together in one place.

The challenge is that this purchase involves a lot of money, a third party (the instructor) who has to confirm, and DMV rules about the vehicle used in the driving test. Each of these uncertainties is a reason for students to give up.

My role

What I ownedThe full booking flow: search (list, map and by name), instructor and driving school profiles, choice of vehicle, package and time slots, payment, confirmation and tracking of lessons and the driving test. 47 screens in a clickable prototype, with variations by license category (A, B and AB).
What I did with the teamConfirmation, refund and deadline rules defined with the business. Changes validated in review meetings (Oct 6 and Nov 6). I was the only designer, with one developer implementing.

Key decisions

Decision 1

A flow that adapts to the license category

What I sawMotorcycle (A), car (B) and car + motorcycle (AB) change the vehicle, the packages and even the order of choices. A single generic flow would leave AB students lost.
Options consideredSeparate flows per category, or a single 3-step flow (vehicle and lessons → time slots → payment) that adjusts to what the student chose.
What I chose and whyA single 3-step flow with variations by category. For AB, students choose car and motorcycle in the same order and book lessons for each on the same time-slot screen.
Decision 2

Installments as an incentive to buy more lessons

What I sawThe package price is a burden. At the same time, more lessons mean more confidence on test day, and a bigger order for the business.
Options consideredFixed installments for everyone, or interest-free installments that increase as the order grows, shown as progress.
What I chose and whyThe “Unlock installments” card: with +1 lesson, students unlock 3 interest-free installments, with the installment amount (“2x R$ 264”) highlighted. This version was chosen on Nov 6, among other button and card options.
Decision 3

Make clear what happens after paying

What I sawThe instructor has to confirm the order. Without clear deadlines, students don’t know whether the lesson is happening or whether they’ll get their money back.
Options consideredManual confirmation by message, or explicit states in the app, with deadlines and automatic refunds.
What I chose and whyOrder sent with a response deadline: if the instructor doesn’t confirm within 3 hours, the refund is automatic. For the test vehicle, cancellation with a full refund within 7 days, and support reaches out if the instructor doesn’t schedule within 90 days.

Solution

Outcome

The app is already published (5K+ downloads, 4.8 rating on the Play Store). The booking flow is being tested in the development environment. What has been delivered and what will show whether it works:

4,8 ★app rating on the Play Store, with 5K+ downloads
47screens in the clickable prototype, with variations for categories A, B and AB
In testingin the development environment. Next: conversion from search to paid order, and lessons per order

Learnings

What I’d take to the next projectA single flow that adapts to the category (A, B, AB) was more sustainable than creating separate flows.
