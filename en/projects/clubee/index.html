Clubee · Loyalty app for local businesses

I designed both sides of the app: customers, who collect points and redeem coupons at neighborhood stores, and businesses, which sign up and track redemptions.

Summary
My roleProduct Designer
Duration12 months
ToolsFigma and FigJam
Year2024–2025
ProblemClubee wanted to simplify the relationship between customers and local businesses, with points and coupons that are easy to use on both sides.
OutcomeComplete interface for the customer and business versions, from onboarding to point redemption and from sign-up to the store’s home.
In this case studyContextoVersão do clienteVersão do estabelecimento

Context

The project had two phases. The first was the customer version: onboarding, choosing categories, the store screen and point redemption. The second was the version for businesses registered on Clubee, so each one can manage its own store and offers.

What I ownedThe UI design of both app versions in Figma, with flows organized in FigJam.
Two sides, one brandClubee’s yellow and bee appear in both versions, but each has its own home: points and stores for customers, summary and activity for businesses.
