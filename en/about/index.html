About me

Hi, I’m Larissa.

I’m a Product Designer based in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, with 2.5 years in the field. Today I design education products, from public-exam prep to math tutoring, covering the whole journey: from information architecture to checkout.

I work closely with developers and with the people who shape the business. I document decisions, test flows in clickable prototypes before deciding, and use AI every day to explore more options in less time.
